always gotta do some sappy bs on my notes app every time my birthday creeps up lol.

when i was 16 my depression was at its worst. slowly lost purpose, let go of passions, unconsciously distanced myself from people thinking there was sum wrong w me. all of my thoughts & habits compounded and i felt like i was stuck in quicksand. life gets harder to live when there’s weights tied to your chest

**1 year ago-now**
17 i started feeling like myself again. started doing small things i enjoyed like getting food late at night or exploring the city more. before i was like an amalgamation of everything i watched, trying to be unique by being multiple people at once. when i realized that, i figured i would reset, and start from pre-quarantine, a time when i didn’t care about anything but my hobbies. had to build myself from the ground up, start working out again after quitting swimming year round. but i’m here, feeling more like me

**what i may miss**
this the part where i yearn for my childhood i’ll obviously miss being a kid. not having that many responsibilities, using my time to play minecraft all day, learn useless things & do random stuff w your friends bc you’re just living & being a kid.
i feel like when you achieve something as a minor it’s generally more well-received than if you were an adult, so i guess i’ll miss that.

but honestly i feel like i’ve been 17 for too long sometimes. when i rebuilt myself as described earlier, i felt like i was re-igniting that childhood spark, and really that’s all i need. growing up i always wanted to act much older than what i was, so slowing down and appreciating my present life was a nice reminder that everything was fleeting aside from all this, i haven’t really been taking my birthday that seriously. been procrastinating party planning, honestly all i want is a family dinner bro
