i always wanted to be a content creator for two reasons.
    i wanted people to see my worldview, my humor and persona through an editing style formed by hours of content i’ve watched over the years. i wanted to build a community where people could be seen and heard for their own taste. i purposefully made it so my channel wasn’t stuck to one style, i was constantly trying to evolve and use different digital subgenres of content.

when i made a youtube channel in 2019, these were my goals. those initial years were probably the happiest i’d ever been, seeing my channel grow but more importantly connecting with people all over the world. having a friend group where we could talk about bedwars, adobe premiere pro crashing, or our own situations.
some of my best friends are really just out there, somewhere
gradually, the uploads became sparse & inconsistent, and at some point i threw in the towel.

i haven’t done anything content related for a while because i’ve neglected my real life so much, and it’s all felt so lonely & isolating. everything i’ve learned socially has come from movies or being muted in discord calls, so every conversation i have feels like hell on earth. people look at me like i’m new to the world, and some decide to tear me up about the things i do, the clothes i wear, the songs i like.

in a way this account really just a last resort to be seen and heard, bc god knows i’m beyond tired of this, something gotta change i honestly just miss my friends
