yesterday i was having dinner with some friends i met at my state college, where i was taking classes at this summer. when we started talking about future plans, i told them i was moving to a different state. they asked why, and i gave them my typical reason of wanting to be in a different environment.

however, they were really curious & kept asking questions about cost and why i shouldn’t transfer to a better in state school. again, i told them i just wanted a new experience and it wasn’t that much more expensive. after a lot of back and forth + a lil judgment, they understood. now i’m lying down thinking about whether or not it’s worth it.

as much as i love atlanta, i really want to try living somewhere else for a couple of years, travel around more, and better understand what i like. more importantly, i want to be far away from people who have either wronged me or i have wronged. people who have silently or loudly judged. but sometimes i think about whether leaving is the move. logically speaking, i should just stay here, take advantage of my residency, be smart. why even bother moving?

it’s so funny because in high school, people were often looked down upon for transferring or going to state school. i fell for that trap, and now i get weird looks when i talk about moving. it’s this constant cycle of judging, and it brings up questions in my mind:
is what i’m doing the objectively “right” decision?
is this what i want for myself?
why do people pay attention when you’re feeling doubt? when you’re down?
