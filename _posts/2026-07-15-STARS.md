of all the songs trojan horse has to offer, stars has to be one of the most interesting songs on the album, especialy due to the 1st half. now that more time has passed, i have two separate thoughts regarding the song: either the distortion represents raw thoughts, or this first half serves as a social experiment. hear me out ;-; ;-;

when matt proxy did his ug cypher verse, it was commonly inferred that he was rapping about the current direction ug music was going, delving into a distorted, rabid whirlwind in the end of the verse. so when i heard STARS, my initial thoughts were what mr. proxy said on his cypher verse. over time, i've seen many people talking about how the song is "the future of music" while others heavily criticize it for its noisy sound profile. with these clashing opinions, it led to my own personal inference that the first half of the song could be a social experiment

i say this because why would he make the first half of his song similar to what he showed to be the potential future of ug music? especially on such a serious project such as trojan horse? maybe it's to see how people choose to perceive a song beyond the sonic experience, or simply if they fw it or not
it's interesting to see the perception of ug music in a similar fashion as modern art
pretty sure i'm wrong ab this but if i was right on it that would be cool too

but if i'm speaking on STARS sonically, i'd say the first half serves as an amazing introduction to the heavenly performance that is the second half
looking at the transcribed lyrics, matt starts off talking about having money, drugs, fame, being in the back of the stu.. 💔
but towards the end of the first half he talks about losing his girl, screaming his final "i came up fr," and abruptly transitioning into his reality via his grandma's call, asking about him. i've been enamored by this whole album
