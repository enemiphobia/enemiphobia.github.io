 usually everything should feel bittersweet, but the moments before graduation have felt more bitter than sweet in a sense. i’m not sure if the success of getting my diploma will ever outweigh the depressive episodes and feelings of being left behind, having to cling to people you thought you were close with, as well as the various instances of racism i have encountered throughout my time here. the constant questioning of why you can’t do things right. ok but i’ll eventually find my people right?
 
 one thing i will miss from this school is all the connections i made with not just close ones, but the small conversations with people i had never talked to up until this year. for a moment it had felt like i really belonged, and it did a good job of keeping me in that mindset. been made fun of so many times it just became a part of my identity. these words don’t describe how much of a hell it was to have to figure myself out & feel like i’m losing everybody. people were laughing because i didn’t have a dumb honor cord but honestly it doesn’t matter because i know what i went through just to make it.
 
 just need to have hope for the future, real hope that it’ll all work out and it won’t be a repeat, and i won’t be sitting there like in 10th grade when i wasn’t planning on making it to graduation
