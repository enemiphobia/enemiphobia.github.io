a couple years ago i met someone online who is like the personification of “that one cousin your parents always bring up when scolding you.” dude is an academic phenom, gym-goer, very chill & witty, objectively handsome, pretty much the kinda guy that makes you wanna do better. we would talk and play valorant alongside other people, but what i noticed with myself is that i started replicating some of his mannerisms in attempt to be cool like him.

whether it was being abnormally quiet, or intentionally trying to sound “more chill” (ig by changing the way i vocalize things), over time i kinda lost my own spark that made people like me in the first place. it really hit me when i spoke to some irl’s i hadn’t talked to in ages. i wanted to put on an act that made me look way more “grown” or mature than i was, and i ended up damn near embarrassing myself.

i tried super hard to fit in a certain mold based off the people i look up to online & offline that it turned me into some mime jester who can’t entertain anybody because they can’t speak!!!!! (purely exaggerating here but i think you get my point) anyway the obvious lesson is to be yourself, but what i learned afterwards was that there’s so many untapped qualities about yourself that are left locked.

in other words (in terraria terms) it’s like starting a melee playthrough and finding out all these subclasses like yoyo-only or true melee that you can play as well for endless amounts of fun
