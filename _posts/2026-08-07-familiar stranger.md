there’s this man i’d see in the corner of my eye, he’s been there as long as i can remember. his face gleamed like burnished bronze, unafraid of what anyone thinks, god-fearing, working tirelessly. he may appear stern, but there’s all love there, and tender injuries from his past.

my first soccer game kinda sucked, someone got hurt and no one scored a goal that night. my tired eyes couldn’t help but look at the bleachers, where i saw that figure in his polo and old straight-leg jeans, happy to see me. i smiled back, knowing he was in my position at some point. he was much better than me, but it’s whatever

i got a pet goldfish from the carnival, which i named darwin after my favorite show at the time. i was afraid to even leave my house, in fear something may happen to him. every day i’d feed him, and i gave him a friend named rose. when they passed, i couldn’t stop crying to my mom about it. the tears washed away, and all i could see from my peripheral was that shining face, working in the shadows. he lost someone too, someone i never met, but i felt that pain as well.

my first day of high school was really scary, the environment around me was a pale azure, tainted by the cloudy world. i’d see him there, sitting in the drivers seat. one time he accidentally ran a red light. it's ok, we can only laugh :)

he wasn't perfect. when i visited my uncle, i'd overhear them yelling at each other, my uncle speaking with envy, and the man with pure anger. they had some rough history, and my uncle definitely had a deceptive past. i can tell the man doesn't speak with anger though, he's just tired after a long day of work. maybe he needs to watch some tv and drink beer.

my mom had two daughters, one of them not here anymore. when the doctors announced she was having a baby boy, the man was shining brighter than ever, knowing there will be someone like him in the world. “i think he’ll turn out okay with you in his life”
